# Changelog

## v0.1.0

1. **Windows from the keyboard.** Halves, corners, the whole screen, the middle, larger and smaller and the next display, each on a shortcut of its own, with Rectangle's shortcuts to begin with.
2. **Tiled.** Every window on a screen in rows, in columns, or in a grid that fills the screen.
3. **Restore.** A window goes back to where it was before Mado moved it, and one Mado placed takes back its size when you drag it away.
4. **Snap areas.** Drag a window to an edge or a corner of the screen to place it there, with its footprint shown first as frosted glass.
5. **The menu.** Every action from the menu bar, each with its picture and shortcut, and any app left alone with Ignore.
6. **Your shortcuts.** Each can be recorded anew or cleared, on one page with the snap areas.
7. **Eight languages.** English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German and Spanish, following the system's.
